Bumblebee

Bumblebee is one of the most beloved Autobots. Known for his bravery, loyalty, and speed, he serves as a scout and messenger for Optimus Prime. Despite losing his voice in battle, Bumblebee communicates through radio signals and music, making him a fan-favorite character. His yellow and black color scheme resembles a bee, and his alternate mode is typically a Chevrolet Camaro.
